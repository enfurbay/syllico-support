Privacy Policy for Disfluency Counter: Syllico

Effective September 30, 2026 · Developer: Enes Furkan Bayram

What Syllico does

Syllico is a manual counting and documentation tool for speech-language professionals. It does not require a separate account and contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

Samples and recordings

Counts, anonymous client codes, notes, visits, templates and optional audio are stored on your device. Recording is optional and requires microphone permission. Imported audio is copied into the app’s storage. Please obtain appropriate permission before recording and avoid unnecessary identifying information in client codes or notes.

Private iCloud backup and sync

When available and enabled, Syllico uses Apple CloudKit to back up and sync your workspace, including audio, in your Apple Account’s private iCloud database. The developer does not receive or access your private workspace. This feature depends on your iCloud settings, available storage and connectivity. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. You can manage the feature in Syllico’s iCloud settings and your device’s iCloud settings.

Files you choose to share

PDF reports, CSV exports, copied summaries and portable backups contain the information you select. They leave the app only when you choose a sharing or storage destination. Portable backups can include audio and recently deleted samples. Choose a private destination and follow your organization’s requirements. Copied summaries are marked for local use and expire from the clipboard after five minutes.

Retention and deletion

Samples remain in your workspace until you delete them. Deleted or discarded samples remain recoverable in Recently Deleted for 30 days unless you permanently delete them earlier. Expired items are removed when the app next performs its cleanup. Synced deletions are applied when connected devices next sync. Files you export or send to others are outside Syllico’s control; delete those copies separately. Apple manages retention of its own service backups.

Device authentication and reminders

Optional app lock uses Apple’s Face ID, Touch ID or device-passcode authentication. Syllico does not receive biometric templates or your passcode. Optional reminders are scheduled locally after notification permission. They do not include client details.

Support

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address and the content you send. The in-app support draft includes your message and app/iOS versions, without automatically attaching samples or recordings. Support messages are used to respond to your request. Please do not include client names, clinical notes or recordings. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing enfurbay@gmail.com, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. Syllico does not receive your payment-card information.

Professional use

The app is intended for professionals and is not directed to children. You are responsible for the information you enter, recording permissions and your professional and organizational privacy obligations. This policy does not claim certification under any particular healthcare privacy standard.

This support website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service under its own privacy statement. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms to this website.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact Enes Furkan Bayram at enfurbay@gmail.com. This policy may be updated when app practices change; the effective date above will be revised.
