Start counting

Create a new session, choose syllables or words and your counting protocol, then start. Use Fluent and Disfluent for each unit. Pause whenever you need to; Undo and Redo correct recent taps. Finish session opens the summary.

Review audio

Open Session tools → Audio to record with permission or import a sample. Use the waveform, playback speed and A–B repeat controls to review it. Counting is manual. Rate is based on active counting time, so slowed audio does not provide natural speech rate.

Export a report

Open a saved sample for PDF or CSV sharing. Use the report builder to select sections, add your practice name/logo and prepare multi-sample reports. Save to Files if no email app is configured.

Keep your workspace safe

Changes are saved locally. Settings shows the iCloud backup status. Use the same Apple Account on your devices, enable iCloud and check available storage. Sync waits while counting or recording is active. Keep an additional private file backup for important work.

Recover a sample

Open Settings → Data & privacy → Recently Deleted. Samples are recoverable for 30 days unless permanently deleted earlier. Full backup files can be restored from Data & privacy.

Purchases and refunds

Syllico is a one-time paid app without subscriptions or in-app purchases. Apple handles purchases and refund requests through Report a Problem.

Clinical scope

Syllico supports observation and documentation. It does not provide a diagnosis or standardized severity score. Follow your selected protocol and professional judgment.
